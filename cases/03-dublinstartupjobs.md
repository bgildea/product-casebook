# DublinStartupJobs.com: running a niche marketplace for 11 years

**Role:** Founder
**When:** 2011 to 2022

## Context

In 2011 Dublin's start-up scene was growing fast, but start-up roles were mixed in with corporate and recruiter listings on general job boards. Small companies needed affordable reach, and candidates who specifically wanted start-up jobs had nowhere focused to look.

## Problem

Start-ups needed credible reach to people who wanted to work at start-ups. Candidates needed one place to find real start-up roles.

## What I did

- Built and ran a niche two-sided jobs marketplace for Dublin and Irish start-up roles
- Owned the whole loop: product, employer relationships, content and the tech underneath
- Kept it running for eleven years alongside other roles

## Why I closed it

By 2022 the big platforms had taken the market. LinkedIn and the general boards had made start-up roles easy to find and cheap to post, which removed most of the reason a niche board existed, so I closed it.

## The marketplace questions this kind of product forces

| Question | Trade-off |
| --- | --- |
| Allow recruiter listings? | More listings and revenue, but dilutes what candidates came for |
| Charge employers or keep it free? | Revenue versus listing volume in a small market |
| Grow beyond the niche? | Bigger audience, but loses the reason to exist |

## Skills this shows

Marketplace fundamentals, long-term product ownership, running a product end to end as a founder.
